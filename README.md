### 🚀 Flagship Engineering Case Studies

#### 1. [FinTech Transaction Fraud & Risk Analytics Engine](https://github.com/dhiliban-r)
> **Google BigQuery • Advanced SQL • Python • Looker Studio**
* **Scale & Optimization:** Modeled **6,362,620 rows** in BigQuery. Implemented custom partition keys and clustering algorithms, cutting scan volumes and improving query speed by **65%**.
* **Risk Discovery:** Audited **8,213 fraud events (£9.6B+ volume)**; proved traditional rules (>£200k) failed to capture **99.8% of fraudulent attacks**.
* **Pattern Isolation:** Identified a deterministic signature where **99.1% of fraud cases** executed full account liquidation, driving **£4.8B** in zero-balance drains.

[Raw Stream: 6.36M+ Txns] ➔ [BigQuery Date-Partitioning] ➔ [Windowed Risk Detection] ➔ [Executive BI Mart]

#### 2. [NHS Outpatient Operations Intelligence Platform](https://github.com/dhiliban-r)
> **MySQL 8.0 • Relational Data Modeling • Statistical SQL • Looker Studio**
* **ETL Architecture:** Designed a 3NF relational data warehouse consolidating **110,527 encounter records** across 81 healthcare facilities.
* **Simpson’s Paradox Resolution:** Uncovered that aggregate data masked campaign efficiency; segmented analysis proved SMS notifications generate up to **+7.44% higher show-up rates**.
* **Predictive Utilization:** Quantified attendance decay (from **4.65% same-day** to **32.59% at 15–30 days**) and formulated a **15% predictive overbooking model** to optimize medical staff bandwidth.

[110.5K Encounters] ➔ [Relational Normalization] ➔ [Lead-Time Cohort Slicing] ➔ [Capacity Recovery Engine]
